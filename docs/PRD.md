# Product Requirements Document

# Unified UPI Autopay Tracker & Manager

## Overview

The Unified UPI Autopay Tracker is a web-based platform designed to help users identify, track, and manage recurring UPI payments across multiple payment applications such as Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm.

The platform analyzes transaction data, detects recurring payment patterns, and provides a centralized dashboard to monitor subscription expenses and autopay mandates.

## Problem Statement

With increasing adoption of UPI payments in India, users frequently enable autopay mandates for subscriptions such as OTT platforms, music apps, utilities, and memberships.

However:

- Autopay mandates are distributed across multiple UPI apps
- Users often forget active subscriptions
- Unexpected recurring deductions occur
- Managing or cancelling mandates becomes difficult

Currently, no unified platform exists that provides centralized visibility and management of UPI autopay subscriptions.

## Objectives

- Detect recurring UPI transactions
- Identify subscription/autopay payments
- Provide a centralized dashboard for all subscriptions
- Help users understand monthly recurring expenses
- Improve financial transparency and control

## MVP

- CSV upload
- Transaction parsing
- Recurring payment detection
- Subscription dashboard
- Monthly spending calculation

## Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if it can:

- Correctly identify recurring subscriptions
- Display subscription insights clearly
- Help users understand recurring expenses
- Provide a centralized subscription management experience
